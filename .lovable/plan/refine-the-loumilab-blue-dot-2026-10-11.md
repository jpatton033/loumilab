# Refine the Loumilab Blue Dot

## Direction
Treat the Blue Dot as a reusable brand device, not a replacement for ordinary punctuation. It will appear only two or three times on a typical page, with each appearance moving the story forward.

The homepage will follow one restrained narrative: **idea → products → impact**.

## Homepage changes

1. **Restore normal punctuation**
   - Replace the blue period at the end of the opening sentence with a normal text period.
   - Stop automatically converting headline periods into blue dots.
   - Remove blue dots appended to the Design, Build, Innovate, and Secure labels.

2. **Create one signature section transition**
   - Refine the existing product transition into the main storytelling moment.
   - A single dot will emerge after the opening, move with a fluid path, and divide with purpose to introduce Orders and Local: one studio creating distinct products.
   - Keep the motion clean, brief, and visually connected to the sections it introduces.

3. **Add one image-led “idea to impact” moment**
   - Use the dot once around selected photography as a moving guide or reveal—not as decoration.
   - The dot will travel from an idea-focused point into the visible work or people, then resolve before the next section.
   - Avoid masking important faces, text, or product details.

4. **Use one restrained directional interaction**
   - Keep a subtle dot response only on a selected high-value action or transition.
   - Remove the generic dot beside every button, the corner dot on every card, and repetitive link treatments.
   - Existing blue hover color and focus rings remain intact.

5. **Keep identity moments distinct**
   - Preserve the blue period inside the official Loumilab wordmark, including its header animation.
   - Keep the footer wordmark calm and static so it closes the page without adding another effect.

## Motion and accessibility
- Scroll-trigger each signature moment once per page view.
- Use Loumilab’s existing smooth easing and light premium visual style.
- Keep layouts stable while motion runs; the dot never blocks clicks or reading.
- With reduced motion enabled, show a simple static transition cue or omit the decorative motion entirely.
- Verify the complete flow on phone and desktop, including reload, scrolling, keyboard focus, and reduced motion.

## Technical notes
- Simplify the shared dot components and CSS rather than layering more variants onto the current system.
- Remove punctuation-specific and repeated hover/card APIs that no longer have a meaningful role.
- Keep semantic tokens for all color and motion styling.
- Scope this refinement to the homepage and shared footer; the official wordmark animation remains unchanged.
