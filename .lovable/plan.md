# Senti hero: "The Awakening"

## What visitors will see
A dark, slow-moving film of a gold, electrically lit brain that loops every 24–30 seconds without a visible restart. It plays in five moments:
1. **First signal:** a faint gold spark in the dark, with dust catching the light.
2. **Formation:** neural pathways come out of the dark and form a brain, side-lit and partly in shadow.
3. **Connection:** a close-up of signals meeting and lighting up pathways.
4. **Reveal:** the camera pulls back to the whole brain, and many pathways light up briefly.
5. **Living system:** a calmer pulse that eases back into the opening.

The headline, supporting line and both buttons stay the same.
- **Desktop:** text on the left, brain on the centre-right.
- **Phone:** a separate tall version of the film, with the brain in the upper half and the text below it. This is not the desktop film cropped.

## How it will be made
Real film footage, with a light interactive layer on top (the approach your brief prefers):
- **Key stills:** I'll create the key stills for each moment in gold, amber and champagne on black, with no blue or purple. One still matches the first and last frame so the film loops seamlessly.
- **Film:** I'll generate one continuous 24–30 second shot from those stills, then make a separate tall phone version.
- **Re-shoots:** if a clip warps or flashes too much, I'll fix that one problem and generate it once more. I'll tell you if a scene still falls short.
- **Interactive layer:**
  - The scene shifts slightly as the pointer moves.
  - Faint gold sparks drift near the pointer and settle back when it stops.
  - The film never pauses or skips because of the pointer.
- **Scroll exit:** as visitors scroll, the brain pulls back and dims, and the next section rises out of the dark. Scrolling is never held or slowed.
- **Fallbacks:**
  - A high-quality still shows while the film loads.
  - Visitors who turn off motion get the still only.
  - The film pauses when it's off screen or in a background tab.
