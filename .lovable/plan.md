# Homepage refinement — cinematic hero and floating gallery

Homepage only. Keep the logo, off-white / deep-black colors, "We build what's next." and blue only for the Dot.

## 1. New hero (three layers, starts moving on load)
- **Background video:** a full-screen looping clip, darkened so the text reads clearly. Direction: a documentary-style, calm scene of real people at work in well-composed spaces. Examples: a ceramicist's studio in raking window light, an architect at a long table, a shop owner checking an order at a counter. No laptop-and-coffee shots and no sci-fi. Plan: 3 short clips (about 8 s each) joined into one seamless loop.
- **Text:** "We build what's next." in bold sans-serif with the Blue Dot. Below it: "Thoughtful technology, distinctive digital experiences, and software built for what's next." Two buttons: Explore Our Solutions and See What We Build. No italics.
- **Moving previews in front:** 3–4 floating panels that slide in from the right, overlap slightly at different sizes and tilts, then drift out. They follow the storyboard: website designs → software screens → Orders and Local → back to the start with no visible jump. No dots or arrows. Pace is about 6–8 s per scene. Panels pause when the hero is off-screen and stay still for people who turn off motion.

## 2. New preview images (instead of screenshots of loumilab.com)
- **Website designs (6):** made-up but polished sites for a hospitality brand, an architecture studio, a florist shop, a law firm, a fashion retailer and an editorial magazine.
- **Software screens (4):** an operations dashboard, a scheduling tool, a workflow builder and a security monitoring view. These are built as live screens in code, so numbers and statuses change on their own.
- **Real products:** the Orders dashboard, the Orders store builder and a Local listing.
- **Removed from the homepage:** the screenshots of our own pages (services, pricing, resources, The Dot., workflow, email). They remain on the pages where they belong.

## 3. "What we build" with a different look for each area
- **Websites:** one large site preview with smaller ones overlapping it.
- **Custom software:** a wide dashboard that updates itself.
- **AI & automation:** a workflow diagram where steps light up one after another.
- **Cybersecurity:** a calm system-status grid with an activity timeline. No padlock icons.

## 4. Floating gallery (later on the page, separate from the hero)
- A deep-black space where 9–12 previews float at different distances. Near ones are larger and sharper; far ones are smaller and softer.
- They move slowly on their own. Pointing at one brings it slightly forward and shows its name.
- It looks different from the sideways rows.

## 5. Text style
- Remove the italic serif accents from the homepage and use bold sans-serif everywhere.
- Headings are separated by size, weight and spacing.
